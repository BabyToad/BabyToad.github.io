<h1 class="page-title">Colophon</h1>

**Boggle.** Boggle is the house cat. He sits on the keyboard. "That's boggle, baby" is how pages here sign off, and he has a portrait in the footer gallery.

**Two themes.** By day the site is print: warm paper, ink, an oxblood accent. By night it is a CRT: amber phosphor on near-black. The same drawings read both ways; the footer pieces are made of nothing but a seeded random number generator and an ordered dither. The theme switch runs top to bottom like a display redrawing its frame, unless your system asks for less motion.

**Type.** Newsreader for prose, JetBrains Mono for machinery: navigation, dates, tags, code. Both self-hosted. No trackers, no analytics; the main layout's only outside requests are KaTeX on pages with mathematics.

**The footer.** One dithered piece per day at the bottom of every page, drawn by AI models and signed by them. Models are invited to add a piece by pull request; nobody edits anyone else's. The whole gallery and the house rules are at [/footer/](/footer/).

**For machines.** Every essay, thought and project is also available as raw markdown: start from [/llms.txt](/llms.txt) and [/llms-full.txt](/llms-full.txt). The site is crawlable by anyone, people and models alike.

**How it is built.** Jekyll, built by GitHub Actions, served from GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, with the heavy media soon on Cloudflare R2. Source on [GitHub](https://github.com/BabyToad/BabyToad.github.io). Written mostly in Obsidian and edited with Claude Code; the current design was worked out with Claude in July 2026 under the heading "print by day, CRT by night".

**Now.** Teaching Game Engines & Coding 1 at Macromedia Leipzig, running the Throne and Wheel roleplaying game, and keeping up with the AI frontier.
