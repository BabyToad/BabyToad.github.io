The one real debateGraph or grid?

Stencil, Opticals, Downbeat and Drywall want a graph: a riser feeds taps, and taps feed consumers. Slipway, Tidewater and Afterimage want a coarse cell grid of about 0.5 m, holding wet, live, heat and gas.

My call: both, and neither simulates flow. Power is a graph, because blackouts are discrete and authored. Wet, soot, charge and gas live in Plumb's per-floor state texture: events write it, the floor shader shows it, and the shock reads it. Only gas drifts, slowly and cell by cell toward vents. That gives Slipway's grid its one upload and Tidewater's one colour per medium, without a solver.

TrapsWhat we won't build

Fluid, fire or gas solversAll fourteen lanes said this. At 640×360 they read as noise and make outcomes random.
GPU simulation read back by gameplayCompatibility has no compute shaders, and a readback stalls the frame (Slipway).
Real active ragdollIt turns 300 bodies to mush and fights “mass, not rubber” (Armature).
An always-on scanner or detective modeThe forecast shows only while you commit to an action (Readout).
A new saturated hue per stateThe combat tells already own amber, red, blue, cyan and violet, so states live in value and texture (Plumb).
Beat-quantised gameplayPunches would feel late. Only the music follows the sim (Downbeat).

Cornerman's recommendationPackage for Thu–Sat

  1. Power graph and blackout A1 · ~14 h
    The headline. It uses lights, music, AI and optics we already have, and it sells the setting.
  2. Utilities in the walls A2 · ~10 h
    Destruction becomes the way into the other systems. It reuses flood and conduction.
  3. Gas as a room state A3 · ~6 h
    A fuse you can see, set off by the Sparker or your rail. It pays out in the blast juggle.
  4. The floor remembers A4 · ~8 h
    A single state texture that is both what you see and what the rules read.
  5. Chain beats and incident cards B1 + B2 · ~15 h
    The payoff. The building gets the kill, and you get the credit.
  6. The Vermin know their floor B5 · ~7 h
    Enemies teach the chemistry before you use it.

About 60 h across ten lanes, roughly 6 h each, which fits Thursday and Friday. That leaves Saturday for tuning and Sunday for release. F73 and F71 get the full set first; F74 and F75 get power and walls. Whatever you pick, it is still all in-house.
