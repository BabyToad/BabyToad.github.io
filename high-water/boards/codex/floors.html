How to use this sheet

Each floor is a city block. Pick one identity per floor, the details you want to see built, and who holds it; then COPY PICKS and paste the text back to Cornerman. Drywall and Plumb build Stage 2 and the cutaway from the picks.

The tone is Gibson's mundane made present: specific, lived-in, unglamorous, funny-sad. There's no neon here unless someone is paying the electric for it. ODD marks the left-field lines.
