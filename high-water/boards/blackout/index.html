These are captures from the game on Floor 70 (the KETSU fight, played by the scripted pad), with the power cut by the debug trigger and restored four seconds later. The HUD, tells and objective brackets are live. The boot text is placeholder copy.

The sequence (about 1.6 s)

  1. The riser dies. The lamps go out outward from the break, each tube stuttering off and turning to dead glass. A soft dark front rides the same way across the view, and the floor's ambient falls with it.
  2. Five frames of near-black (the DOOM / Raid beat).
  3. Emergency red. The Stage's emergency light (on the UPS circuit) comes up on the black beat, with the tubes around it glowing red. The sun through the windows becomes the key light.
  4. High Water's optics boot. A collapse line opens (CRT-style), a sweep runs down the frame, and the grade comes up under OPTICS // LOW-LIGHT (placeholder). The Vermin fumble out phones and torches: small pale glows that the optics flare. Hitlag makes lookouts blind in the dark.
  5. Power back. The lamps return and the optics hand back through a short overexposure.

In the game: the real blackouts

These are the floors' own triggers, going through Sim's power graph rather than a debug emit. On Floor 70, Stage._black_out(the booth) runs when the collector's booth drops. On Floor 71, the PV core breaks. The look follows Sim.floor_dark_changed, the floor's main lighting as a whole. A local circuit, such as training's services, only stutters its own lamps out, with no optics. The lit props (vending, washers, pachinko, turnstiles and the rest) are on the floor circuit and go dark with the lamps. The palette is Bone, the new default.

The palette is a setting

Jonas: bone first, green second, phosphor as the vision. It is now Options · Night vision: Bone / Phosphor / Green, with Bone as the default. Each option shows a swatch of its ramp, and ?optics=phosphor|bone|green still overrides it for QA. After the jam it moves to the operative's loadout page.

The three palettes

The readability rule

Night vision desaturates the world, never the rules:

  • Kept explicitly by colour: the tells, the hazard states and High Water's emitters. The pass keeps any pixel whose chroma matches one of these and that is bright enough to be emitted light rather than lit paint. That includes Plumb's charge crawl (cold white) and embers (warm white), kept apart.
  • Kept as value and texture: wet sheen and gas haze, which the grade preserves.
  • Never touched: the HUD and the objective brackets, which draw outside the world view.
  • Signs: the sign alpha marker passes through unchanged.
